About Me

Hey- it's Kenzie Herrick! I'm a senior game design and development student with a passion for crafting immersive, atmospheric experiences. My focus lies primarily in environment modeling and game art, where I combine strong visual aesthetics with engaging gameplay systems to tell deeper, compelling stories. I have four years of experience in game design, working independently on projects where I manage all aspects of production, from concept to final execution.

Skills

  • Blender – 3D Environment Modeling & Asset Creation
  • Unity – Game Development & Interactive Systems
  • C# Programming & Gameplay Mechanics
  • Game UI Design & User Experience
  • Adobe Creative Suite – Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign
  • Procreate & Krita – Concept Art & Digital Painting
  • Web & Coding – HTML, CSS, JavaScript, C++, Python
  • Project Management – GitHub, Trello

Experience

I have designed and built stylized, atmospheric game environments in Blender and implemented interactive gameplay systems in Unity. My work spans independent projects as well as collaborative assignments, where I am responsible for both visual and mechanical design. I aim to create games that are not only visually compelling but also emotionally engaging, with mechanics that enhance storytelling.

Passion

I am drawn to eerie, immersive, and thought-provoking experiences that explore psychological storytelling. My goal is to craft worlds that players can lose themselves in, where every visual and gameplay choice contributes to the narrative. Through a balance of simplicity in mechanics and strength in aesthetics, I strive to push the boundaries of interactive media and game design. Some of my favorite game titles include: LIMBO, INSIDE, Inscryption, Little Nightmares, The Last of Us, and A Dark Room!


Let's Connect

Equipped with a blend of creativity and technical skills, I am eager to collaborate, learn, and contribute to projects that challenge and inspire. Whether it’s building interactive experiences, modeling environments, or creating concept art, I am always looking to grow as a game designer and artist.
